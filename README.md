# Ilna

**Your university life, in one place.**

Ilna is a free study website for university students. It keeps each of your courses (slides, lecture recordings, links and notes) in one place, uses AI to turn them into summaries and quizzes, lets you share courses with classmates, and gives you blogs to talk with students at your university, in your college and in your major.

Visit **[ilna-academic.com](https://ilna-academic.com)**. It works in English and Arabic, on phones and computers, in light or dark mode.

Questions or feedback: **support@ilna-academic.com**

---

## Getting started

1. Open [ilna-academic.com](https://ilna-academic.com) and choose **Sign up**.
2. Pick your university, college and major, and use your **university email address**.
3. Enter the 6-digit code we email you. You're in.

Your name, username, university, college and major can't be changed later, so check them before you finish. Forgot your password? Choose **Forgot password?** on the login screen and we'll email you a code.

## Your courses

Open **My Courses** and create a course for each subject. A course holds:

- **Files**: PDF and PowerPoint slides, up to 95 MB each. PDFs open right on the page.
- **Recordings**: record a lecture in your browser, or upload an audio file. It's turned into text (Arabic or English) automatically. Tap any line of the text to jump to that moment in the audio.
- **Links**: websites, videos or anything else you want to keep with the course.
- **Notes**: type them, or draw them by hand on up to 20 pages. Notes open ready to read, with drawings shown as slides you swipe through; press **Edit** to change them. Download typed notes as .txt and drawings as PDF.

## AI summaries and quizzes

In a course, choose **Summarize** or **Quiz**, then pick the files, recordings and notes to use.

- **Summaries** come brief or detailed.
- **Quizzes** are multiple choice, with an explanation for every answer. Take them as often as you like; your best and latest scores are kept.
- Both can be read in Arabic or English, whatever language your material is in.

AI can make mistakes, so check what it writes against your course material.

## Sharing courses

Every course has an **ID** like `K7Q2-M9XD`. Press **Share** in a course to choose who can add it:

- **Public**: anyone you give the ID to can add it, with **Add by ID** in My Courses.
- **Private** (the default): only the people you share it with. You can share with your university, your college, your major, your friends, or specific people.

Courses shared with you wait under **Shared with you** in My Courses, where you can **Add** or **Ignore** them. An added course shows with a **Shared** tag. You can open everything in it and take its quizzes, but only its owner can change it.

Notes in a shared course can always be read there. To let people also download them as files, the owner turns on **Let people download notes** in the Share dialog.

## Blogs

The home tabs are four blogs: **General** (everyone on Ilna), **your university**, **your college** and **your major**.

- Write posts, reply to each other, and vote. Sort by Hot, New or Top.
- Add up to **4 pictures** to a post. Hidden photo details, such as where it was taken, are removed automatically.
- Web addresses become clickable links. To link words instead, like "exam schedule", press **Link** in a post or reply.
- **Post anonymously** if you prefer. Others see "Anonymous" (with a number in each thread) instead of your name, and your university, college and major are hidden too. You can switch between anonymous and named once per post or reply.
- See something that breaks the rules? **Report** it.

## Friends

Click anyone's name on a post to see their profile and **Add friend**, or search by name or @username on the **Friends** page in the account menu. Adding a friend is private: they aren't told. Your friends get a **★** next to their names in the blogs. Click someone on the Friends page to see their profile again; removing a friend asks you first.

## Notifications and settings

- **Notifications** tell you about votes and replies on your posts, courses shared with you, and messages from the Ilna team.
- **Settings** has your account details, password, activity history, storage, appearance (theme color) and the full policy.
- **Contact us** in the account menu sends a message straight to the Ilna team.

## Privacy, in short

- Your email is never shown to other students or searchable.
- Recordings are turned into text on Ilna's own server. Summaries and quizzes are written by Google's Gemini AI from the material you choose.
- No ads, no tracking.
- To delete your account and everything in it, contact us.

The full rules are in **Settings → Policy** on the site.
